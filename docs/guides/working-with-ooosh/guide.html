Working with Ooosh

What we expect, and what you can expect from us

You're freelance, so there's no contract between us. This sets out the standards we expect whenever you work on an Ooosh job. Most of it is common sense.

When you're on a job, you're the face of Ooosh to our customers, the artists and the venue. That's why it matters.

Five things to remember

  1. Be on time, and tell us early if anything changes.
  2. What you see on a job stays private.
  3. No drink or drugs, and no phone in your hand while driving.
  4. Report damage or an accident straight away.
  5. A problem with a job? Tell us, not the internet.
1

On every job

  • Dress for the job. It varies, so ask us if you're not sure, or wear an Ooosh T-shirt.
  • Come on your own. Please don't bring friends or relatives to a job.
  • Running late? Tell us and the customer as soon as you know.
  • Can't do a job you've said yes to? Tell us as early as you can. Never just not turn up.
  • Don't pass a job to someone else without asking us first.
  • A customer offers you work directly? Please talk to us first.
  • Leave the artists be. Don't ask them for photos, autographs or guest list.
2

Keep it private

Our customers trust us with a lot, and we pass some of it on to you so you can do the job.

  • What you see on a job stays private. Don't post about our customers, artists, venues or jobs.
  • No photos of artists or their kit online without asking us first.
  • Don't share what we give you: contact details, spec lists, riders, addresses, anything in the portal.

Unhappy with something on a job? The state of some kit, a venue, a customer? Tell us, not social media. Posting about it is the quickest way to stop getting work from us.

3

Driving

  • No alcohol or drugs before or during a job.
  • No phone in your hand while you drive. If we call or message, pull over somewhere safe to call us back.
  • No smoking or vaping in any vehicle, at any time.
  • Nobody else drives an Ooosh van or uses our kit. Our insurance depends on it.
  • Your licence: tell us straight away about any change: points, a ban, or a medical condition.
  • Plan your own journey. We'll tell you where to be and when. Planning the route, when to set off and where to stop is yours.
  • Don't drive tired. Take breaks on long drives.

Damage and accidents

Tell us straight away, before you leave the site, however small. Take photos. A scratch we hear about on the day is easy; one we find later isn't.

Fines

  • Speeding and bus lane fines are always yours.
  • Parking fines we look at case by case. Sometimes there's nowhere legal to park to load in or out. If that happens, tell us on the day, with a photo if you can.
4

Safety

  • Lift safely. Use the trolleys and ramps, and get help with anything heavy or awkward. Nothing is worth an injury.
  • Follow the venue's rules and its staff: loading bays, hi-vis, curfews, where you can and can't go.
  • Working alone, or late at night? Keep your phone charged, and let us know if anything feels wrong.
  • If you're in danger, call 999. Then tell us.
5

Travel and expenses

Unless we've said otherwise:

  • Getting to and from Ooosh isn't covered. Coming from home to collect a van, for example.
  • Travel to other places usually is. Getting back from Heathrow after delivering a van, for example.

Keep the receipt for anything you want paid back.

Finishing up

Mark the job complete in the portal on the day, then invoice us as the How to invoice guide says.

6

What you can expect from us

  • You're free to say yes or no to any work we offer.
  • The details of each job in the portal: where, when, who to contact, and your fee.
  • Paid on time. We pay an approved invoice on a Friday, 8 to 14 days after we approve it. You can follow it in Earnings.
  • Someone to call if a job isn't going to plan.
  • We'll listen. If something about working with us isn't right, tell us.
7

If it goes wrong

We give work to freelancers who keep to these standards. If someone doesn't, we may stop offering them work, and something serious can mean that happens straight away.

We'll ask you to confirm in the portal that you've read this. If anything in it is unclear, ask us.
