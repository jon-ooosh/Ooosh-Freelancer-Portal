Fire: what to do

The studios and the building, out of hours

If you're looking after the building on your own, for example as a studio sitter, you're the person everyone will look to if the alarm goes off. It's simple, and most of it is common sense.

Five things to remember

  1. Raise the alarm: every room has a call point.
  2. Get everyone out: go into every room and kitchen, and knock on the toilets. Leave everything behind.
  3. Don't use the lift.
  4. Call 999.
  5. Meet at the assembly point, by the front gates on East Street. Don't go back in.
1

If you find a fire

  • Raise the alarm. Press the nearest call point (there's one in every room) and shout "Fire!".
  • Call 999 and ask for the fire service. The address is Compass House, 7 East Street, Portslade, BN41 1DL.
  • There's an extinguisher in every room and a fire blanket in each kitchen. Only use one on a very small fire, if you're confident and you can get out safely. If in doubt, get out.

The main fire alarm panel is by the front doors downstairs. How to sound or silence the alarm is printed next to it.

2

When the alarm goes off

  • Get everyone out by the nearest safe exit. Knock on and go into every room, the kitchens included, and knock on the toilet doors. The doors are soundproofed, so a knock alone may not be heard over the music.
  • Leave everything. Don't stop for instruments, bags or anything else.
  • Don't use the lift.
  • Close doors behind you if you can.
3

Outside

  • Go to the assembly point, by the front gates on East Street.
  • Check everyone is out. Your shift page lists who's in each room tonight. Ask each band if they're all there.
  • If anyone might still be inside, tell the fire service when they arrive. Don't go back in yourself.
  • Nobody goes back in until the fire service says it's safe.
  • Then call us.
4

Afterwards

Write down what happened in the notes at the end of your lock-up checklist: when, where, and what you did. That includes a false alarm.

No smoking or vaping anywhere in the building, by anyone. Smoking is outside.
