Notes on round 3
Where it lives. Same pages staff use (BookOutPage / CollectionPage), opened through the freelancer link. Restyle only when the user is a freelancer, or make it a separate freelancer theme — the staff navy look stays.
Steps. Freelancers already skip "Select vehicle" and "Driver & hire", so they see 4 steps: Fuel & mileage · Photos · Briefing · Confirm. Collection has 3.
Fuel. The 9 eighths stay, as one row of 44px buttons and a gauge bar, instead of a dropdown.
Photos. The 18 angles in walk-around order. The "next up" card prompts one angle at a time and the grid below shows progress. The Next button tells you how many photos are left rather than just being greyed out.
Briefing. The list comes from the van type's settings (Premium shown). Copy is shortened to the thing being shown.
Collection. The existing fuel-drop warning stays, now as an amber banner.

Notes on round 2
Job kind. Label = what's moving + direction, from the job's existing what_is_it field: equipment → Backline, vehicle → Van. If what_is_it is blank (about a third of jobs) the label just says Delivery / Collection and no travel chip.
Travel chip. Backline: "Driving there & back". Van delivery: "One way · train back". Van collection: "Train there · drive back". A rule of thumb, not data — staff could override it later if needed.
By date. Default. Yard-day offers stay pinned on top in both views — they're the only thing that needs an answer. The choice should be remembered on the device.
Start. The "Expected" steer and its ordering logic are kept exactly as they are.
Lock-up / Complete. Same fields, same soft "submit anyway" rule. Yes/No/N/A buttons go from ~36px to 46px.
Till. Layout untouched: just the new colours, type and button heights.
Freelancer portal · visual refresh · mobile
Same portal, same flows — new clothes
Payment / hire-form tokens (DM Sans, plum #A75390, warm neutrals), tightened for daily use. Routes, section order and bottom nav are unchanged. Whole cards tap through; the one action that matters today gets a full-width button.

What changed
Tokens. Sky blue + Inter → plum #A75390, DM Sans, warm neutrals from hireform.css. Radius 16 (vs 20 on hire form) and 16–17px body for a denser daily tool.
Actions. "View details →" links go; the whole card taps through (chevron). Start / Lock up become 52px full-width buttons, today only.
Icons. Emoji → line icons in tinted tiles; one hue per kind of work (delivery/collection, crew, studio, yard), plus a small type label so colour isn't the only cue.
Lists. Upcoming collapses into one grouped card; Completed + Cancelled become two tappable rows.
Unchanged. Section order (Ooosh work above Today), routes, bottom nav order, yard-day accept/decline rules.
